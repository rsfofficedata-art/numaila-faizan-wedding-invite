NUMAILA & FAIZAN – INTERACTIVE WEDDING INVITATION (V2)

Open index.html in Chrome, Edge or on mobile. The first screen has only a large interactive envelope. Tap envelope to unfold the wedding card, begin background music, and reveal the invitation. Scroll to see the scratch-off reveal, wedding dates, locations and contact details.

Soundtrack: audio extracted from the reference wedding invitation video supplied by the user. Some mobile browsers require tapping to start sound, which the envelope interaction provides.

Date assumption: November 20–21, 2026. To change, edit HTML and script.js.

All buttons, RSVP form, calendar download and share control were removed.
